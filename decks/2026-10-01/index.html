Version one

The basics you probably said

  1. Post a photo
  2. Follow people
  3. A feed of the people you follow
  4. Likes and comments

Check these off against the board. That is roughly the app that launched on October 6, 2010.

October 6, 2010

25000signups on day one

And the servers crashed that night

25,000 signed up the first day (TechCrunch, 2012-04-09, techcrunch.com/2012/04/09/instagram-story-facebook-acquisition/). 100,000 in a week, 1 million by December, and the servers crashed on launch night (Systrom interview, TechCrunch, 2011-02-02). Ask: why would a photo app crash?

Twist one · what breaks?

Now three billion people use it

  1. Photos have to live close to youEarly on, on Amazon S3, served through a CDN
  2. Caches, so a post isn't fetched a million timesMemcached and Redis, from the start
  3. Moving 20 billion photos into Facebook's data centersIn 2014
  4. Video has to get cheaper to processA 2022 rewrite cut basic encoding compute by 94%

Take guesses before each press. 3 billion monthly users: announced 2025-09-24 (TechCrunch, CNBC). 2011 stack: Django, PostgreSQL, Redis, Memcached on Amazon EC2, photos on S3 and CloudFront, with three engineers and 14 million users (High Scalability, 2011-12-06, summarizing Instagram Engineering). Move off AWS with 20 billion photos: Data Center Knowledge, 2014-06-27. Video: Engineering at Meta, 2022-11-04, engineering.fb.com/2022/11/04/video-engineering/instagram-video-processing-encoding-reduction/.

Twist two · what do we add?

We get paid when you stay longer

  1. A feed that never endsInfinite scroll: there is no last post
  2. A ranked feed instead of newest first2016: ranked by interest, relationship, and timeliness
  3. More to watchStories in 2016, Reels in 2020
  4. Recommendations from strangersExplore makes 90 million predictions a second

This is the turn. Ask what they would add before each press. Ranked feed and its three signals: Instagram, 2016-03-15, instagram.tumblr.com/post/141107034797/160315-news. Stories: 2016-08-02. Reels: 2020-08-05. Explore narrows 500 candidates to 25 in three passes, 90 million predictions a second (Meta AI, 2019-11-25, ai.meta.com/blog/powered-by-ai-instagrams-explore-recommender-system/). Each of these is a whole team: ranking, recommendations, video, infrastructure.

Twist three

Okay, but is that good for people?

  1. 2018: "You're all caught up"A marker when you've seen every post from the last two days
  2. 2023: 42 states sued MetaAlleging features like infinite scroll were built to hook teens
  3. 2026: a settlement, pending court approvalTime reminders and a daily limit for users under 18

Keep it neutral: these are allegations and a settlement, not findings, and Meta did not admit fault. Caught up: Instagram, 2018-07-02. Lawsuit: 2023-10-24, Minnesota and Virginia AG releases. Settlement: NY AG, 2026-08-26, up to $17.1 billion; under-18 reminders at 60 and 90 minutes, a 2-hour daily limit, no access midnight to 6 a.m. Ask: if you built it, where would you draw the line?
