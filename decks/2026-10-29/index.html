Arduino tables

Before you plug in

  1. Unplug USB before you move a wirePlug it back in only to test
  2. Take the power module off the breadboardToday the UNO powers the board

Careful. Read the sensor's labels first. Vcc goes to +, Gnd goes to -. Swapped, it can break.

These keep the sensor and the board from breaking. With the power module still on the board, the UNO's 5V reaches the module's output.
