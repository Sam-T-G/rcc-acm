Three rules before anyone plugs in.

Arduino tables

Before you plug in

  1. Unplug USB before you move a wirePlug it back in only to test
  2. Take the power module off the breadboardThe UNO powers the breadboard today

Careful. Read the labels on the sensor first. Vcc goes to +, Gnd goes to -. If you swap them, the sensor can break.

These keep the sensor and the board from breaking. With the power module still on the board, the UNO's 5V reaches the module's output.

The photo is the kit's sensor. Point at the labels above the pins: Vcc on the left, GND on the right, seen from the front.
