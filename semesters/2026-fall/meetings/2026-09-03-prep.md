## Block 1: What this club is (12:55, 8 min)

Say it in this order. Do not open with the constitution.

**One sentence.** The ACM Student Chapter at RCC is where students who want to get good at computing
practice together, and it is chartered under ACM, the professional association for computing.

**The four pillars.** These are the four words on the website, and every event fits at least one.

| Pillar | What it means in practice |
|---|---|
| Build | Projects, hackathons, the chapter website, the AI Hackathon we run ourselves |
| Compete | Weekly problem solving, the Southern California ICPC Regional, collegiate hackathons |
| Learn | Workshops, study sessions, guest speakers, help with transfer-level CS courses |
| Connect | Discord, meetings, trips, an alumni network that reaches large tech companies |

**Proof the club is active.** Three facts, all sourced in `docs/00-charter/mission.md`:

- RCC has hosted a Southern California ICPC Regional site since 2000, and RCC teams have competed
  since 1998.
- Fall 2025: 11 members were accepted to Cal Hacks 12.0.
- Active members as of the Spring 2026 ASRCC funding request: 22.

**What members get out of it.** Say this to the room: a team for the contest, people to build with
when a class project is bigger than one person, a project you built to put on your resume, and real
work to point to in a transfer application.

**Open to any major, any level.** The lab program below starts at "never written code" on purpose.
Anyone can join; there is no test to get in.

**Housekeeping, 30 seconds:** meetings are every Thursday 12:50 to 1:50 in this room, Discord is
where the club posts updates and talks between meetings (discord.gg/fM2HbsJyBG), Instagram is instagram.com/rcc.acm.

## Block 3: The lab program and the money (13:11, 12 min)

**The pitch, 3 minutes.** Six lab days this semester. Four stations run in parallel, one per skill
tier, 30 minutes each, on a Raspberry Pi Pico 2 W. Everyone builds something that physically responds
in the room. Tiers: T0 has never written code and runs MicroPython; T1 through T3 run Arduino C++ and
map to CIS-5, CIS-17A, and CIS-17B/C. Same board and same pin map at every station, so what a member
wires at one station works the same at the next.

Name two or three stations, because people remember station names better than the tier list:

- T0: Play Your Riff, Cyclone: Stop the Light, Parking Sensor
- T2: Ringtone That Never Blocks, a bit-packed drum machine
- T3: Snake where the body is a queue, unbeatable tic-tac-toe by minimax, an elevator scheduler (one
  car, eight floors; the two-car dispatch is an unscheduled workshop option)

The ladder means the same build appears at higher tiers, so a T0 member can see exactly
what the T2 version of their own project looks like.

**The money, 3 minutes. Say the numbers straight.**

| Line | Amount |
|---|---|
| Cap | $1,000 |
| Base order | $624.65 |
| Max order | $929.64 |
| Both include | 8.75% tax and a shipping estimate |
| Restock reserve | $150, held until after lab day 2 (kit.md §9); headroom under the cap is $375.35 at base, $70.36 at max |

Six station kits plus spares. Code and wiring are public at
github.com/Sam-T-G/rcc-acm-microcontroller-lab; CI compiles every sketch, so the starters and
solutions are known to build. Do not quote a sketch count; none is recorded in the lab repo docs.

**The decision, 6 minutes.** Tell the room the order-by problem as plain facts: the vendor

go out today either moves lab day 1 or costs $130 to $200 more domestically.

gets made instead.

## Block 4: Seats that are open (13:23, 12 min)

People tune out during officer recruiting, so give each role's weekly time cost before its title.

**Why the club needs these seats, 60 seconds.** RCC requires at least seven fee-paid students
including a President, a Treasurer, and an ICC Representative, plus a full-time faculty advisor. Below
that the club is not recognized, and an unrecognized club cannot book this room or request funding.

**The seats.** RCC and ACM use different names for the same jobs. One person holds both titles.

| RCC title | ACM title | What it costs per week | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| President | Chair | Runs or delegates the meeting, owns the semester plan | open |
| Vice President | Vice Chair | Events, Club Rush, ready to run the meeting when the Chair is out | open |

| Secretary | Secretary | Notes within 48 hours, keeps the records tidy | open, optional under ACM |

| ICPC coach | - | Faculty or staff, cannot be a contestant | open |

| Warm-up lead | - | One 15-minute problem, rotating | open, best first task |

Say both of these out loud:

- Chair, Vice Chair, and Treasurer must hold ACM Student Membership, $19 per year. Say the number.
  People who find out about the fee later feel misled.
- The ICC seat means every Monday morning, and three absences cost the club its standing, so it is
  the seat most likely to cost a club its recognition. Say that plainly. Someone whose Monday morning is already free

**The on-ramp: small jobs first.** No one has to run for an office today. Ask instead for the small

1. Run one 15-minute warm-up problem this semester. One problem, one board, 15 minutes.
2. Back up a lab day. Standing next to the person running it, doing nothing but handing out kits.
3. Take notes at one meeting.
4. Then, and only then, an officer seat.

## Deck: built 2026-09-03

The deck exists: `2026-09-03-deck.html` beside this file (single HTML file, no build step; arrow keys,
N for speaker notes, O for overview, F for fullscreen), published at
https://claude.ai/code/artifact/51376fbe-7639-4949-a47d-64d584a73e28. Seventeen slides; the copy and
speaker notes are the run sheet above, slide by slide. The scaffold below is what it was built from.

**Constraints from the ACM design system** (`docs/04-brand/design-system/`, branch `design-system`,
PR #2):

- Slide canvas 1920 x 1080, light theme (`assets.md` §10).
- Orange `#ff4d00` leads. ACM Blue `#0182ac` is a brand color and never a UI color.
- Faces: Inter, JetBrains Mono for anything code-shaped.
- Device: the Rail, **once, on the title slide only** (`assets.md` §10.5, and "once per screen" in
  `accessibility.md` §2.4). The deck has no single pillar, so the Rail runs solid with no Break. The
  only permitted repetition is the short Rail on a callout, at most one callout per slide.
- `tokens.css` is the shipping stylesheet. Copy values from it, not from any earlier showcase page.
- Slide floors from §10.5: title 72 px, body 40 px, ledger line and footers 32 px, code 40 px. One
  idea per slide. The Board (dates for the next four weeks) is one slide, one line per dated thing.
- "ACM @ RCC" as real text on the title slide and the final slide (`bright-lines.md` §1.4).

**What changed from the earlier draft:** an earlier draft of this file had the Rail's Break moving by
pillar as the deck advanced. That breaks the once-per-screen rule. The deck shows where you are with
the ledger line and a progress mark with no hue instead.
