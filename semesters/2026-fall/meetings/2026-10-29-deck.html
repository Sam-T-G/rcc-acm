Three rules before anyone plugs in.

Arduino tables

Before you plug in

  1. Unplug USB before you move a wirePlug it back in only to test
  2. Take the power module off the breadboardToday the UNO powers the board
  3. Read the sensor's labels firstVcc goes to +, Gnd goes to -

These three prevent the problems we can't fix in the room. Vcc and Gnd swapped can damage the sensor. With the power module still on the board, the UNO's 5V reaches the module's output.
