How does a sensor see a car?

Sensor

How the sensor sees a car

  1. One eye sends a beep too high to hearPin 12 tells it when
  2. The other eye hears the echoPin 11 reports how long it took
  3. A longer wait means farther away58 microseconds is 1 cm
  4. Closer than 10 cm counts as a carThe starter code does this math

Fills in on its own. One press shows the rest. The next press moves on.

HC-SR04 datasheet (ElecFreaks): 5 V, 15 mA, 2 cm to 4 m, a 10 microsecond pulse on Trig starts a reading, the Echo pin stays on as long as the echo took, and microseconds divided by 58 is centimeters. It wants at least 60 ms between readings.

The sound goes there and back, so 58 microseconds per cm already counts both ways.
