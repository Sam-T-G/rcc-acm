## Seed ideas (facilitator only)

Use these only if the room goes quiet.

1. **Smart parking garage.** Extend the 10-29 gate: one sensor per spot, a count of open spots, a sign that shows it. The ESP-32 boards could post the count to a web page.
2. **Elevator scheduler on real hardware.** The lab repo has a tested elevator challenge (`rcc-acm-microcontroller-lab/challenges/t3-07-elevator-scheduler`) with measured results for one and two cars. A spring team builds it with steppers or servos and races the algorithms.
3. **Feed or follow-suggestion engine.** Take the 10-01 "who to follow" and Reels questions and build them for real on made-up users and watch logs.
4. **ICPC practice league.** A weekly Kattis problem set through spring, so RCC fields stronger teams at the next regional.
5. **Spring hackathon team.** Pick a spring hackathon [TBD: which one and its dates] and form a team ahead of time.
6. **ACM AI Hackathon on campus.** It's still a Fall 2026 goal in `goals.md` and never got a date. A planning team could run it in spring.
7. **Lab challenges written by members.** Members who did the Arduino labs write the next station challenges for new members.
8. **Club website.** rccacm.com has open work (PRs #9 and #10, the community section). [TBD: Sam's call on whether members can take this on.]

Hardware nobody has used yet (from `lab-inventory.md`): 2 Mega 2560 boards, 6 ESP-32 boards, 3 UNO R4 WiFi, 1 L298N motor driver, 5 extra stepper motor sets. Any project that uses these needs no new purchase.
