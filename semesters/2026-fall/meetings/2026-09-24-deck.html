Some roles we'll look at

Newer jobs, and what they actually do

  • Forward deployed engineer

    Work inside a customer's company and build what gets their AI working.

    Photo: Slashme, CC0
  • DevOps and platform

    Build the tools and pipelines other engineers use to test and ship code.

    Photo: PiDatacenters, CC BY-SA 4.0
  • AI engineer

    Build real products on top of AI models, then keep them running.

    Photo: William Hook, CC BY-SA 2.0

Forward deployed engineer: Palantir came up with the role; OpenAI and Anthropic now run ventures staffed with FDEs to get their tech into big companies (TechCrunch, 2026-07-30, techcrunch.com/2026/07/30/forward-deployed-engineers-are-the-ai-industrys-latest-talent-obsession/).

DevOps and platform: Google's 2025 DORA report says 90% of organizations have adopted at least one platform and ties platform quality to how much value a company gets from AI (Google Cloud blog, 2025-09-23). CNCF calls platform engineering a natural evolution of DevOps (2025-11-19).

AI engineer: LinkedIn's Jobs on the Rise 2026 ranks AI engineers as the fastest-growing role (Dice, 2026-01-14). Do not quote a percentage; none was confirmed.

The photos show the kind of place or tool, not the people.

Some roles we'll look at

Keeping AI honest

  • Evals engineer

    Write the tests that show whether an AI model is actually good at a task.

    Photo: Shiunu, CC BY-SA 4.0
  • AI red teamer

    Try to trick and break AI systems on purpose, so problems get fixed first.

    Photo: Dsimic, CC BY-SA 4.0

Evals engineer: Anthropic has a live posting for Research Engineer, Model Evaluations, to "build the evaluations that tell us, and the world, what Claude can actually do" (job-boards.greenhouse.io/anthropic/jobs/5198255008).

AI red teamer: Microsoft set up its AI Red Team in 2018 and says it has red-teamed more than 100 generative AI products (Microsoft Security Blog, 2025-01-13).
