# Calendar - Fall 2026

Every dated thing this semester, in one table. Deadlines that repeat every year are pre-seeded with `[TBD]` dates. Replace the date when you know it, mark the status `n/a` if the row does not apply this term, and add rows for anything else with a date.

`scripts/new-event.sh` appends a row here for each new event folder. Keep the comment line under the table.

Types: `deadline`, `meeting`, `event`, `training`, `milestone`, `break`. Status: `planned`, `submitted`, `done`, `n/a`.

Academic dates are from the RCCD 2026-2027 academic calendar and the RCC dates-and-deadlines page (checked 2026-08-24). The term runs August 24 to December 18, 2026. Meetings are Thursdays; the first Thursday of the term is August 27 and the last Thursday before finals is December 10.

## Look ahead

- Winter 2027 session: January 4 to February 11, 2027. The club has no winter folder; decide in the decision log if one is needed.
- Spring 2027: classes begin February 16, 2027; spring break April 12 to 18; finals June 4 to 11; term ends June 11. The FY 2027-28 ASRCC packet comes out in early February 2027 and is due in late April 2027.
