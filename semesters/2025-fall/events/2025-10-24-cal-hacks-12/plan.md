## Logistics

- Attendance method: headcount at departure and at the venue; the traveler list stays with the advisor, the repo keeps the count. See [attendance tracking](../../../../docs/02-operations/attendance-tracking.md).
- RSVP or sign-up: Cal Hacks application (regular deadline October 1, 2025) plus confirmation by the Cal Hacks deadline. Club-side sign-up: [TBD].
- Room setup: n/a.
- Food: Cal Hacks provides meals, snacks, and drinks at the venue. Per diem covers travel days and meals outside the venue ($69 travel day, $92 full day).
- Materials: school ID and a government ID (both required at the door), laptop and charger, headphones, change of clothes, toiletries. From the Cal Hacks FAQ.
- Accessibility: [TBD].
- For trips: flights ONT to SFO ($180 per person); 2 cars (what they covered: [TBD]); ground transit about $320 for the group; Cal Hacks ran a free bus from Montgomery BART to the venue; AirBnB for 3 nights ($332.98 per person); per person total about $542 before per diem. Who drove: [TBD]. Emergency plan: [TBD].
- Cal Hacks rules that shaped the plan: teams of up to 4; every team member in person; only hackers accepted and confirmed by the deadlines get in, no exceptions; a quiet sleeping room exists at the venue and hotels are within a 10-minute walk; $100 travel stipends went to first-year university students only, first come first served.
