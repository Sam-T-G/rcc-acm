# Calendar - Fall 2025

Every dated thing this semester, in one table. Deadlines that repeat every year are pre-seeded with `[TBD]` dates. Replace the date when you know it, mark the status `n/a` if the row does not apply this term, and add rows for anything else with a date.

`scripts/new-event.sh` appends a row here for each new event folder. Keep the comment line under the table.

Types: `deadline`, `meeting`, `event`, `training`, `milestone`, `break`. Status: `planned`, `submitted`, `done`, `n/a`.

This calendar was backfilled in August 2026. Dated rows come from the Cal Hacks 12.0 site, the ICPC Southern California Regional 2025-26 page, and the trip budget. RCC academic dates for Fall 2025 were not pulled into this repo; take them from the RCC 2025-26 academic calendar.

- Club renewal, ICC meetings, and good standing: [club-recognition-and-icc.md](../../docs/02-operations/club-recognition-and-icc.md).
- Academic calendar (first day, holidays, finals): the RCC academic calendar page at <https://www.rcc.edu/academics/academic-calendar.html>.
- Elections: [elections.md](../../docs/00-charter/elections.md).
- ICPC Southern California Regional 2025-26 (date, venue, registration window, fee): <https://scl.na.icpc.global/2025-26/> and the [ICPC regional site playbook](../../docs/03-playbooks/icpc-regional-site.md).
- Cal Hacks 12.0 (dates, venue, application deadline): <https://www.calhacks.io/> and the [hackathon trip playbook](../../docs/03-playbooks/hackathon-trip.md).
