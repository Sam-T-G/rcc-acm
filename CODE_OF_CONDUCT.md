# Code of Conduct

ACM @ RCC adopts the **ACM Policy Against Harassment** as its code of conduct. Read it here:

<https://www.acm.org/about-acm/policy-against-harassment>

It applies to every chapter meeting, event, trip, and online space (Discord, Instagram, this repo). It covers everyone present, member of ACM or not.

## What the policy says (short version)

The ACM policy prohibits three kinds of conduct:

1. Abuse: threatening or intimidating conduct.
2. Discriminatory harassment: harassment based on protected characteristics.
3. Sexual harassment.

Harassment can occur when there is no deliberate intention to offend. Consequences can include removal from an activity, exclusion from future events and from leadership, and suspension or termination of ACM membership. The policy was adopted by ACM Council on June 21, 2018.

## Chapter expectations

On top of the ACM policy, the chapter asks the following of everyone in its spaces:

- Treat beginners as beginners. Nobody gets mocked for not knowing something.
- Keep competition inside the contest. Trash talk stays friendly and stops when asked.
- Respect the room. BLCIS A-210 is a shared lab; leave it the way you found it.
- On trips, follow the host event's rules and the trip lead's calls on safety and curfew.
- In Discord, follow the server rules pinned in the server. Moderators can mute or remove anyone who breaks them.

## What happens after a report

1. The officer or advisor who receives the report writes down what was reported, when, and by whom (kept off this repo).
2. If someone is in danger or an event is being disrupted, the officer on site can remove the person from the activity immediately.
3. The Chair and advisor decide on chapter-level consequences (warning, removal from an event, removal from Discord, exclusion from leadership) and tell the reporter what was decided.
4. For action under the ACM policy, the written report goes to ACM. ACM reviews it (President, CEO, or COO), its Committee on Professional Ethics decides, and appeals are heard within 30 days. Details: <https://www.acm.org/about-acm/reporting-unacceptable-behavior>.
5. Nothing about an individual report is ever committed to this public repo. See [docs/01-governance/privacy-and-public-repo-policy.md](docs/01-governance/privacy-and-public-repo-policy.md).
