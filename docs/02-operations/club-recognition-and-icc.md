# Club recognition and ICC

RCC clubs exist because ASRCC recognizes them, and recognition has to be renewed every semester. Lose it and the club account is frozen, campus rooms are off limits, and the club cannot sponsor events. This page is the checklist to stay recognized.

Sources: [Clubs and Organizations](https://www.rcc.edu/life-at-rcc/clubs-and-organizations.html), [ASRCC Student Government](https://www.rcc.edu/life-at-rcc/student-government.html), and the [Club Advisor's Guide (PDF)](https://www.rcc.edu/assets/documents/life-at-rcc/asrcc/forms/Club%20Advisor%20Guide.pdf). The guide carries 2010 and 2019 revision stamps; where it conflicts with the web page, the web page is newer.

## What a recognized club needs

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Members | At least 7 RCC students who have paid the Student Services Activity Fee. |
| Required officers | President, Treasurer, and ICC Representative, drawn from those members. |
| Advisor | At least one RCC full-time faculty member. |
| Constitution and by-laws | On file and approved by the ASRCC Senate. See [constitution.md](../00-charter/constitution.md). |
| Club Packet | Submitted to Student Activities each semester (see deadlines below). |

## Recharter every semester

1. Get the New & Returning Club Registration form. It is a Microsoft Form linked from the [ASRCC page](https://www.rcc.edu/life-at-rcc/student-government.html).
2. Fill the Club Packet: officer list, advisor, meeting time and room (Thursdays, 12:50 to 1:50 PM, BLCIS A-210), and the member roster the packet asks for. The roster goes to Student Activities only; the repo keeps counts, never names or IDs ([privacy policy](../01-governance/privacy-and-public-repo-policy.md)).
3. Submit within the deadline. The web page says within the first six weeks of the Fall semester (or of the semester you are chartering in). The older Advisor's Guide says before the end of the fourth week. Aim for week four and you satisfy both.
4. Confirm with Student Activities that the packet is on file. Write the confirmation date in the semester `README.md`.

Without the packet on file, the account is frozen and the club cannot use rooms or hold events.

## Constitution rules

- New clubs submit a constitution within four weeks of the first meeting.
- Any change to the constitution goes to the ASRCC Senate within two weeks of the change.
- Changing the constitution is a logged decision. Record it in [decision-log.md](../01-governance/decision-log.md) and update [constitution.md](../00-charter/constitution.md) in the same PR.

## Good standing checklist

Run this at the start of every semester and again at midterm.

- [ ] Club Packet submitted and confirmed by Student Activities
- [ ] Senate-approved constitution on file (and any change filed within two weeks)
- [ ] Regular meeting scheduled and actually happening (Thursdays, 12:50 to 1:50 PM, BLCIS A-210)
- [ ] ICC representative named and attending ICC
- [ ] ICC representative has fewer than three absences this semester
- [ ] Advisor is a current full-time faculty member and knows the meeting schedule
- [ ] At least 7 fee-paid members on the roster

## ICC (Inter-Club Council)

ICC is where every chartered club sends one representative. It is also where funding training, event announcements, and club-to-club coordination happen.

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Meets | Mondays, 9:30 AM, Bradshaw Center, Hall of Fame (per the ASRCC page; confirm at the start of each semester) |
| Who | ICC Director, Assistant Director, Secretary, and one representative per chartered club in good standing |
| Our representative | [TBD: role listed in MAINTAINERS.md with consent] |
| Absence rule | Fewer than 3 absences per semester. At 2 absences the Assistant ICC Director notifies the advisor and president. |
| One club per rep | A representative may not represent more than one club. |
| ICC advisor | A Student Activities Coordinator |
| Contact | ASRCC.ICC@rcc.edu |

The ICC representative's job in this repo: after each ICC meeting, post anything that affects us (deadlines, forms, Club Rush dates, funding training) in the officer channel and add dates to the semester `calendar.md`.
