# Reimbursements and spending

Club money at RCC sits in two places: the ASRCC allocation (approved line items from the [funding request](./asrcc-funding.md)) and the club's trust account (money the club raised or was given). Both are spent through Student Activities, not through a club bank account. The only form we have a name for is the Budget and Trust Requisition Form, a Microsoft Form linked from the [ASRCC page](https://www.rcc.edu/life-at-rcc/student-government.html). The rules for it are in the [ASRCC Finance Code (PDF)](https://www.rcc.edu/assets/documents/life-at-rcc/asrcc/forms/2025-2026/asrcc-finance-code.pdf).

## Before anyone spends a dollar

- [ ] Is the expense on an approved line item in this semester's [budget.md](../../semesters/2026-fall/budget.md)? If not, it is a trust account expense or a personal one.
- [ ] Ask Student Activities whether the purchase can be made by requisition (college pays the vendor) instead of a member paying and waiting to be reimbursed. Requisition first, reimbursement as a fallback.
- [ ] Get the advisor's OK in writing (email is fine).
- [ ] Log purchases over the amount the officers agree on in [decision-log.md](../01-governance/decision-log.md). Threshold: [TBD, officers decide and record it].

## Requisition (college pays the vendor)

1. Treasurer fills the Budget and Trust Requisition Form with vendor, amount, line item, and event.
2. Advisor approves or signs as the form requires.
3. Student Activities processes it. Lead time: [TBD, ask Student Activities].
4. Treasurer records the amount against the line in `budget.md` as "committed", then "paid" when it clears.

## Reimbursement (a member paid first)

Only when a requisition was not possible. Confirm with Student Activities in advance that the expense will be reimbursable; a receipt for something ASRCC does not fund will not be paid back.

1. Keep the itemized receipt. It must show the vendor, the date, the items, and the total. Card statements alone are not receipts.
2. Submit through the Budget and Trust Requisition Form or whatever route Student Activities specifies. Exact form and required attachments: [TBD, ask Student Activities].
3. Advisor approval as required.
4. Treasurer records it in `budget.md` when submitted and again when paid. Turnaround: [TBD].

## Receipt rules

| Rule | Reason |
|---|---|
| Itemized, with price and date visible | Hackathon travel stipends (MLH organizer guide) require it, and the treasurer needs it to match the receipt to a line item. Treat it as required for ASRCC too until Student Activities says otherwise: [TBD]. |
| Photograph or scan the same day | Paper receipts fade and get lost. |
| Store copies in the club's shared drive, not in this repo | Receipts carry names and card fragments. The repo gets figures only ([privacy policy](../01-governance/privacy-and-public-repo-policy.md)). |
| One expense, one line in `budget.md` | Date, amount, line item, status. That is the whole ledger. |

## Travel

Trips (hackathons, campus and company tours) are the largest line items and the easiest to get wrong.

- Book with a requisition where the vendor accepts it (lodging often does). Ask Student Activities which vendors they have paid before.
- Per-person costs go in the event plan; per diem rates, if ASRCC applies them, come from Student Activities: [TBD].
- Members who pay their own way still hand receipts to the treasurer if any part is reimbursable.
- RCC paperwork for off-campus travel: [TBD, ask Student Activities and the advisor].

## Trust account

- The trust account is the club's own money, held by the college. Deposits and withdrawals go through Student Activities.
- Old accounts can lose their signers when officers graduate. At every handoff, confirm the current officers and advisor are the signers on file ([handoff-procedure.md](../01-governance/handoff-procedure.md)).
- Balance goes in `budget.md` each semester. Account numbers never go in the repo.

## Fundraisers

ASRCC does not fund fundraisers. Fundraiser costs and income both go through the trust account. Ask Student Activities what approvals a fundraiser needs before planning one: [TBD].

## Who does what

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Treasurer | Owns `budget.md`, files requisitions, chases reimbursements, reports balance at officer meetings |
| Advisor | Signs and approves, holds the receipts folder in the shared drive |
| President | Approves spending outside the budget, logs it as a decision |
| Any member | Hands receipts to the treasurer within a week |
