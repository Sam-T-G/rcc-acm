# Communications

Where ACM @ RCC talks to members and the campus, who is allowed to post, and how often. The announcement template is [templates/announcement.md](../../templates/announcement.md). Brand rules (logos, names, the parent org's usage rules) are in [docs/04-brand/brand.md](../04-brand/brand.md); the full list of links is in [links.md](../04-brand/links.md).

## Channels

| Channel | Address | Audience | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Discord | https://discord.gg/fM2HbsJyBG | Members | The hub. Meeting reminders, event announcements, questions, project chatter. |
| Instagram | https://www.instagram.com/rcc.acm/ | Campus and prospective members | Event flyers, recaps, Club Rush. |
| Parent org platform | ACM (Association for Computing Machinery) chapter page (see [links.md](../04-brand/links.md)) | Members and the wider network | Official event listings and anything the parent org expects to see. |
| Classroom announcements | Through faculty, coordinated by the advisor | Students in CIS and related classes | Two-line pitch for upcoming events and Club Rush. |
| Campus flyers | Boards approved by Student Activities [TBD: posting rules] | Campus | Event flyers with ASRCC acknowledgement when funded. |
| ICC meeting | Mondays 9:30 AM, Bradshaw Center, Hall of Fame | Other clubs | Cross-club announcements, delivered by the ICC rep. |
| Email to Student Activities | studentactivities@rcc.edu | RCC staff | Forms, funding, questions. Officers and advisor only. |
| GitHub Issues | https://github.com/Sam-T-G/rcc-acm/issues | Officers | Proposals, tasks, decisions. Not a member-facing channel. |

Discord is the system of record for member-facing announcements. If it was not posted in Discord, it was not announced.

## Who posts

| Role | Can post to |
|---|---|
| President | Everything |
| Communications officer (or whoever holds the role this semester, see [MAINTAINERS.md](../../MAINTAINERS.md)) | Discord announcements, Instagram, flyers |
| Event owner | Discord announcements for their own event, after the room is approved |
| ICC rep | ICC meeting announcements, Discord posts about RCC deadlines |
| Advisor | Faculty and Student Activities channels |
| Any member | Discord general channels |

Two people should hold admin on Discord and Instagram at all times, one of whom is not graduating this year. Same rule as the repo. Account ownership moves at handoff ([handoff-procedure.md](../01-governance/handoff-procedure.md)).

## Cadence

| What | When | Channel |
|---|---|---|
| Weekly meeting reminder | Day before, and morning of | Discord |
| Event announcement | T-3 weeks (only after the room approval email) | Discord, Instagram, parent org platform, faculty |
| Event reminder | T-1 week, T-1 day | Discord, Instagram story |
| Event recap | T+1 day | Discord, Instagram |
| Semester kickoff post | Week 1 of the semester | All channels |
| Club Rush push | Week before Club Rush (dates from ICC; Fall 2026: [TBD]) | Instagram, flyers, faculty |
| Officer updates (budget, ICC news) | After each ICC meeting | Discord officer channel |
| Elections and handoff notices | Per [elections.md](../00-charter/elections.md) | Discord |

Do not post more than one announcement a day in Discord unless something changed (room, time, cancellation). Reminders are short; the original post has the details.

## Writing an announcement

1. Copy [templates/announcement.md](../../templates/announcement.md).
2. Fill: what, when, where, who it is for, what to bring, link to RSVP or details.
3. Add "Funded by ASRCC" when ASRCC money is involved. ASRCC requires it ([asrcc-funding.md](./asrcc-funding.md)).
4. Check the [brand rules](../04-brand/brand.md): correct club name, correct logo, and the parent org's required disclaimer if it has one.
5. Post to Discord first, then the other channels. Same wording everywhere; shorten for Instagram.

## Rules

- Room, date, and time are announced only after the 25Live approval email exists ([room-booking.md](./room-booking.md)).
- Photos of people go out only with their consent. Crowd shots from behind are fine; faces are not, without a yes. See the [privacy policy](../01-governance/privacy-and-public-repo-policy.md).
- Never post a member's phone number, personal email, or student ID anywhere, including Discord.
- Cancellations go out the moment they are decided, in every channel the original announcement used.
- Speak for the club, not for the parent org or for RCC. If the parent org requires an independence disclaimer, it goes on every public-facing post (see [brand.md](../04-brand/brand.md)).
- Keep a copy of the final announcement text in the event folder for the retro.
