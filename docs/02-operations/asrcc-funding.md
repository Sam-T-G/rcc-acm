# ASRCC funding

ASRCC (Associated Students of Riverside City College) is the student government. It funds recognized clubs from the Student Services Fee Fund on a yearly cycle. This page is the evergreen procedure. The numbers for a given year live in that semester's `budget.md`.

- Current semester budget: [semesters/2026-fall/budget.md](../../semesters/2026-fall/budget.md)
- The FY 2026-27 request as submitted: [semesters/2026-spring/budget.md](../../semesters/2026-spring/budget.md)
- Line item template: [templates/funding-line-item.md](../../templates/funding-line-item.md)

## Who can apply

Only clubs that are ASRCC-recognized and in good standing. That means the recharter packet is on file this semester, the constitution is Senate-approved, meetings happen on a schedule, and the ICC representative is attending ICC. Details in [club-recognition-and-icc.md](./club-recognition-and-icc.md). Fix standing first; a funding request from a club that is not in good standing goes nowhere.

## The annual cycle

The fiscal year runs July 1 to June 30. The request you write in spring pays for next fall and spring.

| Step | When (every year) | 2026-27 cycle (example) | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| Advisor training and ICC budget training | early March | March 2026 | Student Activities walks advisors and ICC reps through the packet. Send the treasurer and ICC rep. |
| Funding request packet released | early February | February 2026 | The form for the coming fiscal year appears on the [ASRCC page](https://www.rcc.edu/life-at-rcc/student-government.html) under Requests for Funding. |
| Request due | late April | April 29, 2026 | Email the completed packet to studentactivities@rcc.edu. What happens to a late packet: [TBD: ask Student Activities]. |
| Hearing | mid May | May 2026 | A 15-minute Q&A with ASRCC. See "The hearing" below. |
| Allocation notice | by July 1 | by July 1, 2026 | ASRCC tells the club what was approved, line by line. Record it in the fall `budget.md`. |
| Not in the budget? Go to the Senate | starting in August | August 2026 onward | Clubs that were not funded can bring a request to the ASRCC Senate once the new year starts. Senate meets Tuesdays 12:50 to 2:00 PM in the Heritage Room (per the ASRCC page; confirm each year). |

Confirm each year's exact dates from the packet itself. The packet is the authority; this table is the pattern.

## What ASRCC will and will not fund

Funding priority goes to on-campus student engagement. If two line items compete, the one that gets more RCC students into a room on campus wins.

ASRCC does not fund:

| Not funded | What that means for us |
|---|---|
| Fundraisers | Fundraiser costs come from the trust account or member money, never from the ASRCC allocation. |
| Personnel | No stipends, wages, or honoraria paid to people. Speaker travel and meals may be fine; ask Student Activities before you write the line. |
| Instructional equipment | Anything the college would normally buy for a course (lab hardware, classroom gear). |
| Things the college must provide | Rooms, custodial service, campus security, and similar. |

The full rules are in the [ASRCC Finance Code](https://www.rcc.edu/assets/documents/life-at-rcc/asrcc/forms/2025-2026/asrcc-finance-code.pdf) (2025-26 version; check the ASRCC page for the current one).

## Writing the request

1. Start in February. Read last year's request in `semesters/<YYYY-spring>/budget.md` and the retrospective in the semester before it. Cut what did not happen.
2. One line item per event or trip, using [templates/funding-line-item.md](../../templates/funding-line-item.md). Each line needs: event name, expected attendance, location, and a cost breakdown (transportation, lodging, meals, marketing, technology, supplies).
3. Total the lines. The public packet from the 2025-26 cycle is the [ASRCC Funding Request form (PDF)](https://www.rcc.edu/assets/documents/life-at-rcc/asrcc/forms/ASRCC-Funding-Request-25-26.pdf); use the current year's form from the ASRCC page.
4. The advisor reviews and signs whatever the packet requires. Give them a full week.
5. Email to studentactivities@rcc.edu before the deadline. Save the sent copy (figures only, no signatures or personal contact details) into the spring semester folder.
6. Log the decision to submit, and the total, in [decision-log.md](../01-governance/decision-log.md).

Membership counts, past attendance, and photos of events (with consent) make the packet stronger. Pull counts from [attendance-tracking.md](./attendance-tracking.md) data in the semester folders.

## The hearing

- Format: 15 minutes of Q&A with ASRCC. You do not get a long presentation slot; expect questions from the start.
- Who goes: president or treasurer plus one more officer who knows the numbers. The advisor may attend; confirm whether they are expected.
- Bring: a printed copy of the request, attendance numbers from the past year, and one sentence per line item on why it belongs on campus.
- Common questions: how many RCC students benefit, why this cost, what happens if you get half, what you did with last year's money.
- Hearing dates are usually offered as a preference window. Reply the same day you get the scheduling email.

## After the allocation

- Copy the approved amounts into the fall `budget.md`. Mark each line approved, reduced, or denied.
- Spending an approved line goes through a requisition, never a personal purchase first if you can avoid it. See [reimbursements.md](./reimbursements.md).
- Acknowledgement is required. Every event that ASRCC money touches must acknowledge ASRCC: on the flyer, in the announcement, and out loud at the event. Add "Funded by ASRCC" to the [announcement template](../../templates/announcement.md) fields when it applies. Follow the parent org's own rules on how its name sits next to a funder (see [brand.md](../04-brand/brand.md)).
- Track actual spend against the allocation in `budget.md` as you go. The next request will ask what you did with this one.

## If the club was not funded

1. Wait for the fiscal year to start (July 1).
2. Starting in August, ask Student Activities how to get on a Senate agenda.
3. Bring a single, specific ask (one event, one amount) rather than the whole year.
4. Log the outcome in the decision log and the semester budget.
