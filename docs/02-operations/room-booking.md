# Room booking

Rooms at RCC are booked through 25Live, and only the club advisor (or a staff designee) can submit the request. Students are not given 25Live access. Plan around that: every room request starts with an email to the advisor, at least three weeks out.

Source: Facilities Request Procedures in the [Club Advisor's Guide (PDF)](https://www.rcc.edu/assets/documents/life-at-rcc/asrcc/forms/Club%20Advisor%20Guide.pdf). Also see the ASRCC [Event Form Checklist (PDF)](https://www.rcc.edu/assets/documents/life-at-rcc/asrcc/forms/2025-2026/Event-Checklist.pdf) and the Student Activities Event Form linked from the [ASRCC page](https://www.rcc.edu/life-at-rcc/student-government.html).

## The rules that bite

| Rule | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Advisor submits in 25Live, at least 2 weeks before the date | Students cannot submit. Two weeks is the college minimum; give the advisor a week on top of that. |
| Tables, chairs, and setup go in the original request | Adding them later means a new request and a new clock. |
| Two automated emails; only the second is the approval | The first email is a receipt. Do not announce the room until the second arrives. |
| Print the 25Live confirmation and file it with the Event Form | Student Activities wants both together for anything beyond a regular meeting. |
| Advisor attends the event | RCC policy for club events. Confirm availability before you pick a date. |

## Booking a room for an event

Follow this alongside [event-checklist.md](./event-checklist.md).

1. Pick two candidate dates and the room you want (T-6 weeks). Check the semester `calendar.md` for holidays and finals.
2. Email the advisor with: date, start and end times including setup and teardown, room, expected headcount, tables and chairs needed, and any AV or power needs. One email, all the details.
3. Advisor submits the 25Live request (T-4 weeks or earlier; 2 weeks is the hard minimum).
4. Wait for the second automated email. Ask the advisor to forward it.
5. Fill the Student Activities Event Form (Microsoft Form on the ASRCC page). Attach or bring the printed 25Live confirmation. Exact submission route for the form: [TBD, ask Student Activities].
6. If food is served, the advisor files the Food Authorization form. Where to get it: [TBD, ask Student Activities].
7. For events outside normal hours or with outside guests, contact College Safety and Police. How and how early: [TBD, ask Student Activities].
8. Announce the event only after step 4.
