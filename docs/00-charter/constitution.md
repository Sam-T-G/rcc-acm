## Article I. Name

The name of this organization is the ACM Student Chapter at Riverside City College, referred to as "ACM @ RCC" or "the chapter."

## Article II. Purpose

The chapter is a student-driven community around computing education, problem solving, and competitive programming. It exists to:

1. Give RCC students regular practice in programming and problem solving.
2. Field teams for the ICPC Southern California Regional Contest and support RCC's role as a contest host site.
3. Send members to collegiate hackathons and run the chapter's own events.
4. Connect members with each other, with alumni, and with the computing profession through ACM.

## Article III. Affiliation

1. The chapter is a student chapter of ACM (Association for Computing Machinery) and follows ACM chapter policies.
2. The chapter is a recognized student club of Riverside City College under the Associated Students of Riverside City College (ASRCC) and follows ASRCC and Student Activities rules.
3. The chapter is an independent student organization. Its statements do not speak for ACM or for Riverside City College.
