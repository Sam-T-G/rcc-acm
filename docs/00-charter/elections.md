How officers get chosen, and what has to happen in the week after. Dates for the current cycle live in `semesters/<term>/calendar.md`, not here.

|---|---|
| Any office | Enrolled RCC student, Student Services Activity Fee paid, active member ([membership.md](membership.md)) |
| Chair, Vice Chair, Treasurer | ACM Student Membership held before taking office ($19 per year) |

## Who can vote

Active members present at the election meeting. Definition of active: [membership.md](membership.md). Proxy or online voting: [TBD].

## Procedure

1. **Announce.** At least [TBD: two] weeks before the vote, the Chair announces the date in Discord and at a meeting, and appoints a nominating committee (ACM expectation).
2. **Nominate.** Members nominate themselves or others in Discord or at a meeting. Nominations close [TBD: one week] before the vote.
3. **Speak.** Each candidate gets up to two minutes at the election meeting.
4. **Vote.** [TBD: secret ballot on paper, or a form]. A majority of votes cast wins. If nobody has a majority, run off between the top two. Ties: [TBD].
5. **Count.** Two people who are not candidates count. The advisor may observe.
6. **Announce.** Results are announced at the meeting and posted in Discord the same day.

## The week after (checklist)

This is the part that gets skipped, and it is the part that keeps the chapter alive.

- [ ] Log the result in [../01-governance/decision-log.md](../01-governance/decision-log.md): date, offices, "decided by member vote." Names only with each officer's consent.
- [ ] Update `semesters/<term>/roster.md` roles.
- [ ] Update the ACM [Chapter Administrative Interface](https://www.acm.org/chapters/chapter-administrative-interface) with the new officers. ACM cannot accept the annual report unless at least one current officer is listed.
- [ ] Confirm new Chair, Vice Chair, and Treasurer hold ACM Student Membership.
- [ ] Update the club registration with Student Activities (New and Returning Club Registration form, linked from the [ASRCC page](https://www.rcc.edu/life-at-rcc/student-government.html)).
- [ ] Update [MAINTAINERS.md](../../MAINTAINERS.md) and GitHub admin rights. Two admins minimum, one not graduating this year.
- [ ] Transfer Discord server ownership and Instagram credentials to the new holders. Record who holds what in MAINTAINERS.md (accounts, never passwords).
- [ ] Transfer trust account signer access. Confirm with Student Activities that the new Treasurer can actually spend.
- [ ] Start the [handoff procedure](../01-governance/handoff-procedure.md): outgoing team fills `semesters/<term>/handoff.md`, incoming team reviews and merges.
