# Mission

The ACM Student Chapter at Riverside City College exists so that RCC students who want to get good at computing have a place to do it together. We are student-run, open to any RCC student, and chartered under ACM (Association for Computing Machinery).

## Mission statement

Build a student-driven community around computing education, problem solving, and competitive programming, and give RCC students the practice, the teammates, and the connections they need to compete and transfer with confidence.

## Four pillars

These are the four words on the website. Every event should fit at least one.

| Pillar | What it means in practice |
|---|---|
| Build | Projects, hackathons, the chapter website, the ACM AI Hackathon we run ourselves |
| Compete | Weekly problem solving, the Southern California ICPC Regional Contest (RCC hosts a site), collegiate hackathons such as Cal Hacks |
| Learn | Workshops, study sessions, guest speakers, help with transfer-level CS courses |
| Connect | Discord, meetings, trips, and an alumni network that reaches Google, Amazon, and other tech companies |

## What we actually do

- Meet weekly: Thursdays, 12:50 to 1:50 PM, BLCIS A-210.
- Host a site for the Southern California ICPC Regional Contest each fall. RCC has been a host site since 2000 and RCC teams have competed since 1998 (source: [RCC news, 2024](https://www.rcc.edu/about/news/2024-icpc-competition-rcc.html)).
- Send members to collegiate hackathons. Fall 2025: 11 members were accepted to Cal Hacks 12.0.
- Run the ACM AI Hackathon. Date and outcome: [TBD].
- Keep the [website](https://github.com/ACM-RCC/ACMWebsite) and the [Discord](https://discord.gg/fM2HbsJyBG) running.

## How we measure it

Counts, not names. Each semester's `retrospective.md` reports:

- Active members (22 as of the Spring 2026 ASRCC funding request)
- Meetings held and average attendance
- Events run and attendance
- Contests entered and teams fielded
- Members who traveled to a hackathon
- Funding requested and received

## Relationship to ACM

We are an ACM student chapter. That means we file an annual report with ACM every fiscal year, keep at least Chair, Vice Chair, and Treasurer as ACM Student Members, and have a Faculty Sponsor who is an ACM Professional Member. Details in [officer-roles.md](officer-roles.md) and [membership.md](membership.md).

## Relationship to RCC

We are also an ASRCC-recognized club. That means we recharter every semester with Student Activities, send a representative to the Inter-Club Council, and request funding from ASRCC each spring. Details in [../02-operations/club-recognition-and-icc.md](../02-operations/club-recognition-and-icc.md) and [../02-operations/asrcc-funding.md](../02-operations/asrcc-funding.md).
