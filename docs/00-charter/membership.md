# Membership

## Who can join

Any currently enrolled Riverside City College student. There is no application or prerequisite. Show up or join the Discord.

## How to join

1. Join the Discord: <https://discord.gg/fM2HbsJyBG>. Pick up the member role there (see [attendance-tracking.md](../02-operations/attendance-tracking.md)).
2. Come to a meeting: Thursdays, 12:50 to 1:50 PM, BLCIS A-210.
3. Sign in at the meeting. The sign-in sheet or form is how we count you for funding.

## Minimums to keep the club alive

| Requirement | Number | Source |
|---|---|---|
| RCC recognition | 7 students who paid the Student Services Activity Fee, including a President, Treasurer, and ICC Representative | [RCC Clubs and Organizations](https://www.rcc.edu/life-at-rcc/clubs-and-organizations.html) |
| ACM charter | 10 individuals willing to carry out the chapter's mission | [How to start a student chapter](https://www.acm.org/chapters/students/how-to-start-a-student-chapter) |

If the count drops toward either number, say so in the semester retrospective and make recruitment the next semester's first goal.

## What members can expect

- A weekly meeting with a problem set, a workshop, or a talk.
- Help finding a team for ICPC and hackathons.
- A seat in the car (or on the flight) for hackathon trips, subject to acceptance by the host event and to the budget.
- A vote in elections and on amendments.
- The [Code of Conduct](../../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) enforced on their behalf.

## What the chapter expects

- Follow the Code of Conduct.
- Sign in so the count is right.
- If you commit to a trip or a contest team, show up or say early that you cannot.
- Bring problems you are stuck on. That is what the meeting is for.

## Leaving

Stop coming. There is no exit process. Discord roles are pruned at semester close ([attendance-tracking.md](../02-operations/attendance-tracking.md)).
